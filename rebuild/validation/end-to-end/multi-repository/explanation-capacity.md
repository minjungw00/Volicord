Accepted correction, 2026-11-05. This focused evidence completes the capacity work
following `5c3955ae`; it is not official V11, a gate, a semantic-quality verdict or
human acceptance. The user's explicit decision permits separating retained reads

## Cause and selected correction

The preserved accepted Korean Music Deck response used 53,234/53,248 bytes:
7,176 serialized text bytes (7,138 authored UTF-8 bytes), 33,821 citation-object
bytes, 10,294 diagram bytes and 1,943 other bytes. Its 43 citations contained 21
distinct Source bindings. This is a publication representation/capacity pressure,
separate from the historical V11 timing failure. The original untrimmed rejected
draft, rather than a reconstructed baseline, actually requires 53,871 bytes.

The preceding investigation measured a lossless citation-table alternative at
39,691 bytes. It would change the retained wire and require a format/history
transition; it is not adopted as a compatibility decoder or overflow channel.
Existing lossless public-reading binding interning stays unchanged. Details of
that comparison and the independently measured analysis costs remain in
[analysis resource pressure](analysis-resource-pressure.md).

The bounded correction raises the compact realization ceiling by 3 KiB to
**56,320**, and complete retained content/decoder/Privacy body admission by 1 KiB
to **164,864**. Capacity remains
`min(56,320, 164,864 - exact_metadata_reserve)`. It still guarantees 16,384 response
bytes before generation. The final preparation bound (131,072), raw input (65,536),
paragraph character bound (16,384), Source capture, 49,152-byte embedded code
aggregate, per-window/citation/SVG/HTTP/MCP limits and official qualification
thresholds/workload remain unchanged. Refusal reports measured capacity and
preserves the complete explanation rather than recommending prose reduction.

A second obstruction was reproduced before approval: changing budget values alone
made genuine old v9 publications unreadable because `retained_matches` called the
new-publication validator, including the current preparation fingerprint. Requiring
republication would fail when original Source bodies had expired.

Preparation and reading now share one request-owned, nonserialized
`ExplanationBasis` for canonical evidence/status/conflict selection. The producer
adds instructions, exact resource reservations, final JSON bounds and its
fingerprint. New compose/record still requires that current fingerprint and fresh
permitted Source evidence. Retained reading reconstructs the exact canonical
metadata, compares the full retained envelope and applies shared content, language,
claim-hash, role and diagram-seal constraints. Operations retains exact managed,
latest-publication, subject/language/Project, canonical-link and Source-authority
checks on every request. No read builds a producer plan or reapplies generation
reservations as historical freshness.

The original preparation hash remains syntactically checked provenance. Its
historical preimage is not reconstructed or cryptographically authenticated by
this read. Page/realization fingerprints still bind exact read bytes. Canonical
correction, forgetting, explicit deletion/withdrawal, corrupt/unsupported data and
replacement selection retain their effects. No automatic conversion, same-ID
rewriting, body recovery, cross-request body cache, old-policy numeric branch,
format revision or parallel decoder is introduced. Explanation 9 and generated
metadata 11 stay exact-current; unsupported v8 is not revived.
